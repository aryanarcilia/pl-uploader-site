Privacy Policy – PL Uploader

Last updated: 2026-11-25

PL Uploader is a personal tool for uploading videos to the operator's own YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services.

By using this application you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed

With your authorization through Google OAuth 2.0, the app accesses your YouTube account to upload videos, set thumbnails, add videos to playlists, and read basic information about your channel and the videos it uploaded.

How data is used and stored

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the local computer that runs the app.
  • The app stores the IDs and metadata of the videos it uploaded, only to show upload history.
  • No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties.
  • Data is deleted when the job is deleted or the account is disconnected.

Revoking access

You can disconnect your account in the app at any time, or revoke access at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
